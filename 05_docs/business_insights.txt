Business Insights

1. Overall sales performance
The business generated approximately 10.67M in gross sales and 9.77M in net sales after returns.

2. Return impact
Returns/cancellations account for around 896.81K, with a return rate of 8.41%. This means returns have a noticeable impact on final revenue.

3. Country performance
The United Kingdom is the dominant sales market. Other countries contribute much smaller sales values compared with the UK.

4. Sales trend
Sales increased strongly toward the later months of 2011, with a visible peak near the end of the year.

5. Product performance
Top-selling products include real merchandise items such as REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER and PAPER CRAFT products after excluding postage, fees, and adjustment rows.

6. Return concentration
Return value appears concentrated in a small number of products, especially PAPER CRAFT , LITTLE BIRDIE.

7. Data quality impact
Some rows had missing CustomerID values, blank descriptions, zero prices, negative quantities, and non-product operational rows. These were handled using cleaning rules and reporting filters.

8. Business recommendation
Management should monitor high-return products separately and review whether specific products, fulfilment issues, or operational processes are driving return value.
