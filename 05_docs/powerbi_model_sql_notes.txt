Power BI Model SQL Notes

Model-ready fact view:
- v_fact_transactions_model

Dimension views:
- dim_date
- dim_country
- dim_product
- dim_customer

Relationship keys:
- InvoiceDateOnly to invoice_date
- Country to Country
- StockCode to StockCode
- CustomerID to CustomerID

Important modeling decision:
Use StockCode as the product relationship key instead of Description.
Use date-only field for date relationship instead of full timestamp.


dim_product was revised to enforce one row per StockCode because some StockCode values appeared with multiple descriptions in the cleaned product view.
