Working rule:
Exclude postage, charges, fees, manual entries, bank-related rows, Amazon/admin rows, and adjustment descriptions from product-level reporting.

Important note:
These rows may remain in the base fact table, but they should not be treated as real products in product sales rankings or product return rankings.

Business benefit:
This makes product analysis more realistic and prevents operational rows from appearing as top-performing or worst-performing products.
